# Progress Report — 2026-09-05 to 2026-09-14

## Session Summary

This stretch covers everything since the last real commit (`5d7ca28 testing`, 2026-09-05) through today. Three roughly sequential arcs: (1) unifying how Mine Captain/Superintendent/Payroll browse and review completed Operations sheets, plus a self-billed "Contract Shifter" concept; (2) a Spare Shifter promotion mechanism so a Mine Captain can temporarily cover a crew with a qualified Miner; (3) the largest piece — a full architectural rework of how "baskets" (a Crew + Discipline pair, e.g. "Crew A — Development") are represented, capped off by a new **Crew Grid** page that gives the Superintendent a single bird's-eye view of every basket, mimicking his real paper/Excel sheet, with inline add/replace/transfer/move actions and drag-and-drop. Along the way, several real pre-existing bugs were found and fixed as a direct consequence of exercising code paths that had never actually been tested end-to-end (two of them: a hard type-error in "Add Coverage" that had never fired because that form had never been successfully submitted, and a forced `employmenttype` override in Spare Shifter promotion that was a design mistake, not just a bug).

Everything below was verified with a throwaway `timesheets/order_check_test.py` `TestCase` (fake data, isolated test DB) per session, deleted once green — nothing was tested by writing to the real dev database.

## 1. Unified Ops Browsing & Review (2026-09-12)

**Why:** Three separate "browse completed Operations sheets" flows (Mine Captain, Superintendent, Payroll) and one live review flow (Ops Approval Inbox) had each grown their own slightly different drill-down shape over time, and the Inbox had a real bug — the newest one caused a header to visually vanish mid-review.

**What we built:** A shared `month → day → shift → flat table` pattern for all three read-only browsing flows (day tiles show submission counts, not hours; shift-level pages are one continuous HTML table banded by header, Day amber / Night indigo), and a `date → shift → continuous review page` pattern for the live Inbox, reusing the existing approve/reject/bulk-approve logic across every header on a page via a new optional `next` redirect field. Fixed the actual bug: the Inbox's shift-level queryset only matched `overallstatus='Submitted'`, so the moment a Captain approved the first line of a header, it flipped to `'In Progress'` and dropped out of the query — splitting one review session across a disappearing page. Also removed a now-fully-redundant separate "In Progress" grid on the Inbox home page.

**Effect:** One consistent browsing pattern instead of three near-duplicates; the Inbox bug (headers vanishing mid-review) is gone.

## 2. Contract Shifter — self-billed relief shifter (2026-09-12)

**Why:** A Miner sometimes fills in as a shifter on their own login and needs to bill their own hours on the sheet they're filing — but must not also get the normal Shifter's personal-timesheet workflow, which would let the same hours be claimed twice through two different channels.

**What we built:** Reused the existing `User.employmenttype` field (`Staff`/`Contract`) as the switch. A Contract shifter gets a "Yourself" row option on their own Ops sheet, is hard-blocked from filing a personal Business timesheet, and gets a new **My Pay History** page (all their `OperationsEntry` rows company-history-wide, with Paid/Pending/Rejected status) plus matching dashboard stats, replacing the normal Shifter's Business-timesheet nav/quick actions.

**Effect:** No double-claiming path exists; a Contract shifter has one clean self-billing surface instead of two overlapping ones.

## 3. Spare Shifter promotion & Crew Coverage (2026-09-13)

**Why:** A Mine Captain needed a way to temporarily promote a qualified Miner into a real Shifter role to cover for someone, without creating a second account (which would fragment that person's pay history across two `employeeid`s).

**What we built:** A new `User.minerlevel` field (`Lead`/`1`/`2`/`3`/`4`, only `Lead`/`1`/`2` eligible for promotion) and a new seeded "Spare Shifter" role. `crew_coverage` gained a "Step 1 — Promote a Miner" form that flips the *same* `User` row's `roleid`/`shiftertype`/`crewid` in place rather than creating a new account, and a reversal step that flips them back to Miner automatically once their last coverage assignment ends (held back with a warning if they've since picked up a real basket of their own — see §5's demotion guard, which generalized this same protection).

**Effect:** Covering a crew no longer requires a second account or manual role bookkeeping; the promotion/reversion is self-managing in the normal case.

## 4. Shifter & Miner Management + Workforce stats (2026-09-13)

**Why:** The old "Crew Assignments" page name undersold what it does, and the Superintendent had no fast way to bulk-set Miner Level / Staff-vs-Contract for a Shifter or Miner without going through System Admin's full employee editor.

**What we built:** Renamed to **Shifter & Miner Management**, extended to list Miners alongside Shifters with both groups' Miner Level and Employment Type bulk-editable in one save. Added a "Workforce" stats block (Total Shifters / Total Miners) to the Superintendent dashboard.

**Effect:** One page for both bulk classification tasks; a small visibility improvement on the dashboard.

## 5. Basket Architecture Redesign (2026-09-13 → 2026-09-14)

**Why:** Eric's real process (shared via screenshot) organizes work into "baskets" — a (Crew, Discipline) pair, e.g. "Crew A — Development" — each normally run by one Shifter. Two gaps stood in the way of modeling this properly: a Shifter can legitimately run *two* baskets at once during a handoff (breaking the assumption that a Shifter's own `crewid`/`shiftertype` fields are the single source of truth for "which basket is this roster row part of"), and `Position` had no discipline field at all, so position choices couldn't be scoped per basket.

**What we built:**
- `Position.shiftertype` (nullable, classified gradually via a new **Position Catalog** page) so position choices can be scoped to a basket's discipline.
- `CrewAssignment.crewid`/`shiftertype` — basket identity now lives on each roster row itself (mirroring how `OperationsHeader` already stores its own `crewid`/`section` independently of the shifter), auto-stamped from context, never manually picked. Backfilled all existing rows via a data migration. `User.get_active_baskets()` — a shared helper computing every distinct basket a Shifter currently operates (home + picked-up), used by both `crew_assignment_detail` and `new_ops_sheet`.
- **Replace action**, unified to cover three cases from one panel: hire a brand-new Miner, assign an existing available Miner, or move an existing Miner who's active elsewhere (their prior assignment auto-ends).
- **Transfer Basket** action — bulk-repoints an entire basket's active roster to a different existing Shifter; only clears the departing Shifter's home identity if it *was* their home basket.
- `new_ops_sheet`'s "which crew am I filing for" picker widened from one home basket to every basket the Shifter currently runs (Pamela's call: a Shifter running two baskets files two separate sheets, not one combined one) — this also exposed and fixed two latent bugs the multi-basket change would otherwise have introduced: the roster-prefill query wasn't scoped to the specific basket (would have bled one basket's crew onto another basket's sheet), and the duplicate-header dedup check didn't include crew, so two different baskets' sheets on the same date+shift would have collided into one header.
- **Design correction (Pamela caught this one):** Spare Shifter promotion/reversion was unconditionally forcing `employmenttype` to `Contract`/`Staff`, on the flawed assumption every promoted Miner is hourly labor. Removed — the field now carries through unchanged in both directions.
- **Demotion guard:** changing a Shifter's role away from access-level-5 (via role edit, or via Spare Shifter reversion) is now blocked while they still own an active basket, since `crew_assignment_detail` requires access-level-5 to resolve a Shifter and a demotion mid-basket would orphan it. Found and fixed an unrelated bug in the same pass: `crew_coverage.html` had no messages-rendering block at all, so every success/error/warning on that page — including the new guard's warning — was silently never shown.

**Effect:** A Shifter can now genuinely run two baskets without data collisions anywhere downstream; position pickers are basket-scoped; Superintendent has real Add/Replace/Transfer tools instead of only System Admin's blunter employee editor; the promotion mechanism no longer silently mis-classifies people's employment type; a role change can no longer strand a basket.

## 6. Crew Grid (2026-09-14)

**Why:** Once baskets had a real data model, the natural next step was a single page mimicking Eric's actual paper/Excel sheet — one glance at every basket at once — as an **additive** page (nothing existing was removed; `crew_assignment_detail` and Shifter & Miner Management still work standalone).

**What we built, in order:**
- Base layout: A/B/C/D columns, AB/CD rendered merged (`colspan=2`) only in the Longhole/Logistics bands (they're already real `Crews` rows, no new schema needed), Development/Longhole/Logistics/Production bands in order, each position pulled from the now-classified Position Catalog. A "Needs Attention" section surfaces any assignment that doesn't line up cleanly with the grid (missing or mismatched basket data) instead of silently dropping it from view.
- A flat "Mine Captains" reference row and per-crew cosmetic "Room Allocation" text fields (client-side only, no schema).
- **Multi-occupant lanes:** a position can legitimately be double/triple-staffed in one basket (e.g. 4 Water Management people on one Logistics crew) — rather than hiding all but the first, each position gets as many stacked rows as its busiest column needs, with the label printed once via `rowspan`.
- **Inline actions:** Add Member, Replace (existing/new-hire), Remove, and Transfer Basket all moved from "click through to another page" to modals directly on the grid — mechanically, the existing `crew_assignment_detail` POST actions gained a safely-validated `next` redirect field so they land back on the grid instead of navigating away; no business logic was duplicated.
- **Drag-and-drop:** dragging a name onto an empty slot posts a new `move_position` action (same ends-old/creates-new mechanics as Replace's "move" path) — deliberately restricted to empty targets only, since a drag-swap of two people felt too easy to trigger by accident.
- **Assign Shifter:** clicking an "Unassigned" basket opens a modal listing only Shifters not currently running any basket at all (a new `assign_basket` action, the single-target counterpart to the existing bulk classification table) — guarded server-side against ever overwriting someone's existing basket. If nobody's free, a confirm dialog explains why before offering to redirect to Crew Coverage (to promote a Spare Shifter instead) rather than silently jumping there.
- **AB/CD horizontal packing:** since AB/CD columns are already double-wide, overstaffed positions there pack 2 people per row before wrapping vertically, instead of growing straight down like the single-width A/B/C/D columns do.

**Effect:** The Superintendent now has one page matching his real mental model of the operation, with every management action available inline; three real bugs (type-mismatch in the "already there" drag no-op check, a rowspan-vs-flat-count test assumption, a POST-string-vs-DB-int comparison) were caught and fixed while building and testing this.

## 7. Crew Coverage: Superintendent can manage too (2026-09-14)

**Why:** Originally scoped Mine Captain-manages/Superintendent-read-only; revised — the Superintendent can already do the equivalent through the Crew Grid and Shifter & Miner Management, so read-only access to Crew Coverage specifically no longer made sense.

**What we built:** `can_manage` now covers both access levels. Removed the now-unreachable "read-only" fallback message in the template.

**Bug found and fixed along the way:** the "Add Coverage" form submits `employeeid` (matching every other dropdown on that page), but the view was assigning it straight into the FK's raw numeric `eid` column — a hard type error waiting to happen. Confirmed zero `CrewCoverage` rows exist in the dev DB, meaning this path had never actually succeeded before. Fixed to resolve through `employeeid` properly.

**Effect:** Superintendent has full Crew Coverage access; "Add Coverage" actually works now, for both roles.
